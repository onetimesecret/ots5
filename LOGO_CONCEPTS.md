# Ephemeral Messaging Logo Explorations

24 minimalist, high-contrast logo concepts exploring the themes of **disappearance**, **encryption**, and **urgency** for a service delivering sensitive information with absolute ephemerality.

## Design Principles

- **Minimalist**: Clean vector artwork with essential forms only
- **High-contrast**: Pure black and white, optimized for both backgrounds
- **Scalable**: Sharp and legible at all sizes
- **Symbolic**: Evokes irreversible confidentiality without clichés

## Design Philosophy

**What does irreversible confidentiality look like?**

These concepts avoid security clichés (locks, shields) in favor of:
- **Negative space** - the void as protagonist
- **Fragility** - forms that appear to be breaking apart
- **Finality** - motion that suggests irreversibility
- **Transmission & deletion** - simultaneous sending and erasing

Each mark suggests both the transmission of a secret and its immediate, permanent destruction.

## Technical Specifications

- **Format**: SVG vector graphics
- **Viewbox**: 200×200 (scalable to any size)
- **Color modes**: Black-on-white and white-on-black versions
- **Rendering**: Optimized for clarity at small sizes
- **Balance**: Tested for visual integrity under color inversion
