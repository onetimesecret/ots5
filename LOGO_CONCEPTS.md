# Ephemeral Messaging Logo Explorations

**40 minimalist, high-contrast logo concepts** exploring themes of **disappearance**, **encryption**, and **urgency** for a service delivering sensitive information with absolute ephemerality.

## Design Principles

- **Minimalist**: Clean vector artwork with essential forms only
- **High-contrast**: Pure black and white, optimized for both backgrounds
- **Scalable**: Sharp and legible at all sizes
- **Symbolic**: Evokes irreversible confidentiality without clichés
- **Dual-tested**: Every concept validated in light and dark modes

## Design Philosophy

**What does irreversible confidentiality look like?**

These concepts avoid security clichés (locks, shields, keys) in favor of:

- **Negative space** - the void as active protagonist
- **Fragility** - forms that appear to be breaking apart
- **Finality** - motion suggesting irreversibility
- **Duality** - simultaneous transmission AND deletion
- **Temporal precision** - the single moment of viewing
- **Entropy** - order degrading to chaos

Each mark suggests both the transmission of a secret and its immediate, permanent destruction.
