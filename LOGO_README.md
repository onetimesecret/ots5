## Design Philosophy

Every concept explores **irreversible confidentiality** through:

- **Disappearance**: Forms breaking apart, fading, vanishing
- **Finality**: Irreversible motion and destruction
- **Precision**: The single moment of viewing
- **Duality**: Simultaneous transmission and deletion

**No security clichés**: No locks, shields, or keys. Instead, negative space, fragility, entropy, and temporal precision.
